Welcome

This site serves as a personal portfolio created using semantic HTML to demonstrate proper structure and meaningful markup. It introduces who I am, what I am studying, and how I approach learning web development.

The focus of this project is on organization and accessibility rather than visual design. By structuring content thoughtfully, the site becomes easier to navigate and understand for both users and assistive technologies.

What This Site Is About

This website was developed as part of my academic coursework to demonstrate an understanding of semantic HTML elements and content structure. Each page has a specific purpose and uses headings and sections to clearly communicate information.

Over time, this site will grow into a professional portfolio that showcases completed projects, technical skills, and reflections on what I have learned throughout my studies. It represents both my current abilities and my progress as I continue learning.
